i na niej zostają. Ich historia jest w tym repozytorium.

- **Pakietów plików serwerowych**, na których działa projekt: r40250
  („[40250] Reference Serverfile” autorstwa TMP4) i mt2009 (Martysama r41023).
  Obowiązują ich własne warunki.
- **Wycofanego WebClienta** autorstwa AzzlackSyndicate i jego danych. Projekt
  go nie publikuje, nie pobiera i nie wspiera.

  Connector/C, fragmenty Boost i inne). Mają własne licencje, zapisane w ich

### From version 2.2.17: the author's licence

From version 2.2.17 Metin2 Playerbots is covered by the author's licence
([LICENSE](LICENSE)). In short:

- **you may** download the project from its official sources, play it
  privately and with friends, make copies for your own use, change its
  settings, modify it for your own use, and record and stream gameplay;
- **you may not, without the author's written permission,** distribute the
  packages or the code in them, sell the project or access to a server that runs
  on it, or create and publish conversions of it.

The source code of new versions is not published in the repository. The
repository holds the releases with their packages, the changelog and the files
the launcher and the panels read.

### Up to and including 2.2.16: MIT

Versions up to 2.2.16 were released under the MIT licence
([LICENSE-MIT.txt](LICENSE-MIT.txt)) and stay under it. Their history is in
this repository.

### Parts that remain MIT

- **AzzlackSyndicate** (`AzzlackSyndicate/metin2-singleplayer-serverfiles-linux`) —
  the original Linux port foundation, installers, Docker packaging and
  administration panel came from that project. They are MIT-licensed under
  "Copyright (c) 2026 the Metin2 Server Suite contributors", and that notice
  is kept in LICENSE-MIT.txt. The original Git history and commit authorship
  have been retained.
- **Other people's contributions released up to 2.2.16** were released with
  the project under MIT, and their authors keep their rights to them. In newer
  versions the author distributes them under the MIT terms, with their
  copyright notice kept.

### Other authors' contributions

- **seban latino** — the Metin2 Singleplayer Panel (`linux-port/docker/seban-panel`),
  the second panel in the install. It is his project and his terms apply.
- **Iwakura** — the bots' personality system, the price list, the item tiers,
  the shop and guild names, the bots' nicknames and the Community Patches.
- **ĹŌŞƬĒĶ** — the login screen and Discord Rich Presence, the language pack,
  whispered conversations with the bots, the personality row over a bot.
- **Colide** — the client's Auto Hunt window.
- **OskarPWA** — the bot depot window, the skill icons and the F9 GM panel.
- **SIZOWSKI** — the design of the bots' dynamic split between channels.
- **Tyrion** — searching the offline shops for one particular item.
- **Kenny, Pabloo, Mur4s** — fixes to party pickups, to bots' errands in a
  player's party, to the bear quest and to the Teleport Ring.

### What none of these licences cover

- **Metin2.** The game, its source code, data and client belong to **Ymir
  Interactive and Webzen**. Neither the author's licence nor MIT covers them,
  and neither can.
- **The server-file packages** the project runs on: r40250 (the "[40250]
  Reference Serverfile" by TMP4) and mt2009 (Martysama r41023). Their own terms
  apply.
- **The retired WebClient** by AzzlackSyndicate and its data. The project does
  not publish, fetch or support it.
- **Third-party libraries** inside the game source (Python 2.7, LZ4, MariaDB
  Connector/C, Boost fragments and others). They carry their own licences, in
  their own files.

### In practice

- Running a Metin2 private server is, in most jurisdictions, a copyright
  infringement against Webzen, whatever any file in this repository says. That
  is a risk you take, and no file of this project changes it. Do not charge
  for access and do not pretend to be official.
- If you are Ymir, Webzen or another rights holder and want something here
  removed, write on the project's Discord or open an issue. This is a hobby
  project and nothing here is worth arguing about.
